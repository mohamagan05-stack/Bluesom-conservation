Learn and Get Involved in Protecting Somalia's Ocean

Short lessons on coastlines, coral reefs, and marine wildlife — and why protecting them matters.

Why Ocean Awareness Matters

Somalia's coastline is rich in marine life, but it faces threats from pollution, overfishing, and climate change. The lessons below explain the key issues and why they matter.

Coral Reefs

Coral reefs shelter hundreds of fish species and protect coastlines from strong waves. Rising sea temperatures are their biggest threat.

Seagrass Meadows

Seagrass captures large amounts of carbon and provides a home for smaller marine life like shrimp and juvenile fish.

Marine Wildlife

Dolphins, sea turtles, and sharks are key parts of Somalia's marine ecosystem. Their survival reflects the overall health of the ocean.

Plastic Pollution

Millions of tons of plastic enter the world's oceans every year, harming marine life and polluting the country's coastlines.

Sustainable Fishing

Overfishing reduces fish populations. Sustainable practices help ensure there is enough fish for future generations.

Climate Change

Rising sea temperatures cause coral bleaching and sea level rise, which affect communities living along the coast.
