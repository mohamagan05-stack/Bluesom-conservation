WHO WE ARE

For a healthier ocean and a sustainable Somalia.

BlueSom Conservation is a marine conservation organization dedicated to safeguarding Somalia's coastal and marine environments.

Our approach connects scientific research, conservation action, public awareness, and local communities to create practical solutions for the sea.
