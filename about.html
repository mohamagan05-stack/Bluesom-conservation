Conservation, science, education and community action for Somalia's marine future.

OUR STORY

A vision for a healthier Somali coast.

Bluesom Conservation is a marine and coastal conservation initiative focused on protecting Somalia's ocean environment and the people connected to it.

“A healthy ocean is not only a conservation goal. It is a foundation for thriving communities and future generations.”
01

Our Mission

Protect marine and coastal ecosystems, promote sustainable use of marine resources, raise environmental awareness, support research and empower coastal communities.

02

Our Vision

A Somalia where healthy oceans, thriving biodiversity, clean coastlines and empowered coastal communities exist together for generations to come.

03

Our Values

Conservation · Community · Education · Sustainability · Collaboration · Transparency.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Science and local knowledge belong together.

Effective conservation listens to communities, learns from evidence and turns knowledge into practical action. Bluesom is designed to grow through collaboration with researchers, educators, young people, fishers, organizations and coastal communities.
